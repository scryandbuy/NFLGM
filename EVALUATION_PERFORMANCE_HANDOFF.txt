Changes
-------
- valuation.py: explicit, nested-safe comparison batches reuse deterministic
  raw contract comparisons and position/window groups. Each pool is already a
  fixed comparison snapshot. Replacement pools receive independent reads.
  At most four pool versions remain in memory; all are discarded on return or
  exception. Returned value dictionaries are independent. Window RNG draws,
  cap checks, asks, contract terms and other live adjustments remain outside
  this cache. Comparison pools must not be edited in place within a batch;
  the decorated production callers replace them after applicable signings.
- trades.run, market.ai_bids, extensions.ai_round/in_season_round opt into this
  batch lifetime. Existing market quote, roster and retention guards remain.
- roster_needs.py: an assessment retains its planning player dictionaries;
  candidate additions/removals only copy changed position groups. Defensive
  variants share job rankings inside a single proposed roster, while each
  lineup independently reserves eleven distinct players. Candidates, role
  eligibility, weighting, scores and tie ordering are unchanged.
