Kickoff reachability correction (2026-10-07)
Base: 6c26f20. Kickoff-only; no changes to shared receiving/rushing YAC or punt behavior.

Confirmed defect: every breakaway was forced through two tackle contacts selected
by ratings, without checking whether defenders could reach the returner.

Correction: coarse conditional lane geometry gives the cleared inside wave a
trailing position, retains two outside contain players, and uses the actual kicker.
The existing analytic interception solver requires physical reach before a tackle.
It preserves tackle rating contests; beating a reachable tackler is not automatic.
Coverage/return selection and opportunity probability are unchanged.

NFL primary alignment source: 2025 Rule 6,
https://operations.nfl.com/media/ntif5hxb/2025-nfl-rulebook-final.pdf
Coverage begins at receiving 40; kicker at own 35. The candidate samples coarse
post-lane positions, NOT actual tracked coordinates. Contain starts 25-40 yards
from catch before gained yards; kicker safety reference is own 35. These are
simplifications, not measured NFL tracking estimates.

Controlled evidence: 43 tests passed. Faster trailing defenders can catch; slower
trailing defenders cannot; slower ahead/contain defenders can stop; passed contain
is not teleported forward; actual kicker can both stop and miss; short returns do
not enter new geometry. Existing score/penalty/fumble/clock tests pass.

Paired actual-save probe: audit_kickoff_reachability.py, 12000 individual fixed-seed
kicks per variant, actual units and kicker, short-kick bias 0. Both variants had
10115 returns and 308 breakaway opportunities. Baseline 5 TD, candidate 103 TD.
Average 22.14 -> 23.10. Raw report includes all individual scorers.
Candidate Austin Carter (99 speed) scored13 in roughly375 assigned kickoffs;
Anthony Hebert and Dre Kepler6 each. Multiple other players1-5. This is repeated
opportunity testing, NOT a forecast of individual season totals.
Logged contacts:266 reachable contain,240 unreachable contain,168 reachable kicker,
2 unreachable kicker,2464 unreachable trailing players. Trace stops once tackled,
so these are evaluated/logged contacts, not every potential downstream defender.

Full-game gate:16games,155kickreturns,1KR TD,23.199average;no score-conservation
violations. Several existing broad register thresholds missed (see JSON); this
small candidate-only gate does not establish league balance or comparative effects.

Uncertainty: the rate moves substantially (~1.02% in direct probe). No coefficient
was tuned to a touchdown target. Coarse containment sampling, no sideline-out event,
and static pursuit after a broken tackle limit realism. Full-season balance is NOT
verified. Review these explicit limits before integration; do not describe as fully
validated NFL-rate calibration. Existing save/historical results are unchanged.
