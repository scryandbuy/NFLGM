CHECKDOWN ROUTES: SHORT-OUTLET BENEFIT REQUIRES AN ACTUAL SHORT ROUTE
Base: 87ffe02, after the isolated man deep-help correction.

Football motivation and alternatives
A quarterback may work through a progression to a back, TE or WR underneath.
A later read is not automatically a checkdown: a vertical receiver cannot
receive the accuracy benefit of a short outlet merely because he was drawn
late in a randomized progression. If protection keeps the outlet in, the QB
must retain the cost/risk of throwing deeper, scrambling or throwing away.
The existing QB read profile, coaching preferences, imperfect openness reads,
receiver skill differences and defensive response remain in place. There is
no target-share cap or across-the-board reduction to WR1 preference.

Observed evidence
Retained 2026 logs include Howse checkdown/deep/four-verts TD at 21 air yards
(seed 93031, W8 CIN-TEN, drive 9/play 9), and checkdown/deep/dagger/two-man
intended 27 yards (W7 BAL-CIN, drive 6/play 13).

Before correction, a natural direct resolver call (GB-DEN, deep four-verts,
down 1, seed 1) picks WR P0964, labels checkdown, travels 24 air yards and
receives the 1.29 checkdown completion modifier. That initial reproduction
uses raw roster pools; independent matched diagnostics below use genuinely
fielded 11-personnel/nickel units and confirm this is not a raw-fixture artifact.

Mechanism
select_target previously selected either of the bottom two receivers in a
random weighted order for checkdown, without checking route depth or role.
Only fourth-down plays had explicit route_air metadata. On ordinary WR/TE
routes, the checkdown label could keep the medium/deep throw and its large
accuracy bonus. The receiver was not actually on an underneath route.

Correction
- Identify concept flat/back/check jobs before separation and target choice.
  Their existing outlet distance is 2.6 yards and separation uses short depth.
- Record route depth for every eligible receiver; protection's retained
  blockers never reappear as checkdown candidates.
- Checkdown chooses the first actual short outlet in the already sampled,
  player/coach-weighted progression. Explicit route distance takes priority
  over position or a semantic outlet label, including fourth-down routes.
- With no outlet, use an existing deeper progression choice as a second read
  (first when only one receiver exists), without the checkdown modifier.
- Use the selected player's established route depth/air at the throw.
  Do not reinterpret a vertical as short only after selecting a checkdown.
- Explicit fourth-down delayed releases remain short before separation;
  normal conversion routes retain their required depth. Screens/swings retain
  their behind-the-line identity.

Unresolved balance, explicitly not claimed fixed
The broad 2026 leader-share concentration is not solved: leader shares rise
slightly in the direct-call sample. Short-checkdown completion is high at
approximately 89-96% in these three static-roster cases. Correcting geometry
raises completions while reducing air/YAC per catch; the existing accuracy
modifier still needs representative calibration. Neither a new share quota
nor a generic ordinal-rank reduction is justified here. Target priorities,
route opportunities, defense response and aggregate passing balance remain
open for the coordinated review. No full-game scoring conclusion follows
from seven versus six passing TDs at a fixed field position.
