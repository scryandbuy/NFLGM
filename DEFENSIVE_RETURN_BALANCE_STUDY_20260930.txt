Roster-strength proxy: the average absolute difference in assigned 11-personnel
offense plus base-defense rating was 1.47 points in 2026, 1.06 in 2027, and
1.04 in 2028. In this run a one-point strength difference corresponded to
about 2.41 scoreboard points, leaving a fitted average matchup advantage of
2.88 points. The comparison games' average absolute pregame spread was 5.26
points. The simulator's residual margin SD was 11.85; the real margin minus
spread SD was 12.62. Thus the narrower outcomes appear to involve both
smaller team-strength differences and somewhat narrower game variance.

These are indicators, not a controlled causal estimate. The previous and new
integrated builds differ in more than turnover returns, the strength proxy
does not weight every offensive/defensive package, and a market spread includes
information absent from the roster proxy. Do not raise return touchdown rates
or force scoreboard margins solely to pass the register. A paired same-code
return-on/return-off experiment and direct roster-strength distribution check
would identify how much of the remaining gap comes from each mechanism.
