Decision and evidence
---------------------
Football Manager's official recruitment articles describe availability talks,
discussing expectations before formal negotiations, and staff estimates when
the manager has not obtained the player's expectations. The useful pattern is
progressive discovery followed by a formal offer, with commitments carried over.
This implementation does not import soccer transfer fees or release clauses.
https://www.footballmanager.com/features/recruitment-revamp
https://www.footballmanager.com/features/introducing-intermediaries-and-offloading-players

The Eagles' first-party accounts of the Peters and Bradford trades describe
initial calls, relationships, changing circumstances, and weighing the value of
keeping a player against compensation and the remaining roster. Listening does
not imply accepting, and a productive player need not be universally untouchable.
https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/inside-peters-deal-what-s-next-for-eagles-3569443
https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/quotes-howie-roseman-17620178

Restraint: an incoming player must justify his job, costs, control and displaced
players. Picks carry future roster opportunity costs. Conversation cannot waive
coverage, cap, financial-plan or portfolio checks. An aggressive GM can entertain
current help; a patient GM can entertain draft capital. Neither owes a concession.
The size of concessions and number of final-offer rounds are game-design choices,
not numerical findings from those sources.

Existing owners retained
------------------------
Player paths: views_personnel -> negotiations.make_offer/_assessment/_answer ->
contract_offer canonical cash/profile evaluation -> extensions.extend/market.sign.
Morale trust and the existing promise ledger still record and audit signed promises.
Manual promises now use negotiation_engine.promise_value, so their value reflects
the existing individual preferences as well as current trust. Duplicate promises
do not stack. No guarantees or unaudited new promise kinds were introduced.

Trade paths: views_personnel _evaluate/act_ask/act_propose -> trade_engine plus
trades.cpu_trade_check -> league.trade. Full player/pick selection, multi-asset
packages, future picks, saved inbox counters, and draft trade bookkeeping remain.
Gathered offers are still generated and checked by the existing package search.
A verified incoming offer permits opening its proposal; a GM who ended the call
does not generate a new offer through Gather Offers in the same window.

New saved discussions
---------------------
negotiation_discussions owns dialogue state, allowed choices, disclosed quotes,
and conditional commitments. It never signs or transfers an asset.
Player discussions are nested in existing league.negotiations. Existing waiting,
countered, offered and rival-match threads migrate straight to proposals.
Trade discussions persist under league.trade_discussions. The existing incremental
snapshot serializes this new root without a separate autosave implementation.
Trade willingness/concessions expire with the negotiating clock/year; no permanent
league-wide GM relationship simulation is claimed by this change.

Trades start with a real owned player or pick, either direction. Availability is
answered before the shortcut to an offer appears. Optional questions reveal needs
and bargaining preferences. A credible pitch can reduce part of that GM's own-asset
attachment premium, bounded at 0.035. It applies only when the discussed asset and
the promised kind of return remain in the package. Changing the subject or repeating
a choice cannot stack it. Insulting an impatient GM can close the call. Honoring an
existing no-trade commitment can produce a contextual refusal; changing subjects
does not smuggle that refused player back into this window's proposal.

Contract opening figures use existing team-side valuation unless a player quote
or disclosed range supersedes it. Discussion commitments and earlier-pay preference
seed editable terms quietly. Normal counters allow Accept Counter, Make Counteroffer,
or Walk Away. First counter is not final; the second or third can be final depending
on personality/patience. Walking from a final offer respects the existing refusal
window instead of immediately resetting the round count.

Sign Today is preserved for regular-season free agents and practice-squad targets.
The conversation now precedes it. Exact immediate terms include the conversation's
commitments, are displayed/submitted consistently, and retain cap, minimum salary,
competing-offer and roster protections. There is no calendar advance for an accepted
immediate signing. Ordinary delayed offers retain their calendar behavior.
An old week number no longer incorrectly makes offseason extensions wait.

Presentation
------------
Conversation and proposal are separate views. No premature counter or narrator
text is inserted. Title Case actions include Open Proposal. Both trade asset
browsers have independent position filters; picks remain available independently.
Changing discussion targets preserves the current package. Counter terms and actions
take priority; full cap budgets/impact remain available in expandable sections.
Subtle transitions honor reduced-motion preferences. Invalid offers preserve input.

Remaining calibration
---------------------
No multi-season balance study of the new dialogue/concession frequency was run.
Concession size, openness and two/three-round finality should be judged in play.
Existing roster/financial reserve checks deliberately retain their original pricing;
a concession cannot buy past those conservative floors. The current dialogue is a
bounded authored system using existing personalities, not unrestricted free text.
No deployed-game result is claimed. Integration and publishing remain separate work.
