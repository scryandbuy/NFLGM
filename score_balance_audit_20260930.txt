Baseline: integrated 412553d; isolated branch codex/register-balance-20260930.

Scope and rationale
The old passing path resolved pursuit to an endpoint, capped it at the goal, then multiplied the capped distance by congestion.
Final event, score and spot were mutually consistent; this was NOT an awarded game-state touchdown subsequently revoked.
However, non-screen catch-and-run scores with <26.4 yards remaining were impossible after that adjustment, even after beating every pursuer and winning the final race.
Screens could either lose that endpoint or inflate recorded YAC beyond the goal through the additional awareness multiplier.
A simple exemption for resolved touchdowns raised scoring too far on holdout, so it was rejected.

Selected change
Apply the existing congestion factor and existing screen-awareness factor to each pursuit distance increment BEFORE tackle/goal checks.
A defender still gets the next tackle opportunity if congestion keeps the receiver short. A successful final escape reaches the goal.
Runs retain gain_scale=1.0 and their original resolution. No coefficient, rating slope, playcall, clock, roster, penalty, or OT change.
