Designed-read accuracy correction

Change: medium/deep designed reads use normal first-read completion multiplier 1.0. Short, screen and swing behavior, read selection, route geometry and other modifiers are unchanged. Backend and browser mirror match.
Football rationale: a planned downfield read remains valid but must win its downfield matchup; retain quick-throw benefit. No target quotas or scheme/coach homogenization.
