CORRECTED
Personnel
- Pending waiver claims cannot be bypassed by a practice-squad signing.
- Direct demotions follow the same waiver eligibility rules as other releases.
- Reopening negotiations respects the agent's refusal window and team identity.
- Ordinary contract countdown does not fulfill an extension promise.
- Open legacy no-tag promises use the current promise type, without replaying
  old morale consequences.

Coaching and Front Office
- Previous-season comparisons select a genuinely earlier year.
- Coordinator evidence follows the games coached, with minimum evidence and
  proportional consequences for partial seasons. A replacement does not inherit
  the departed coach's full-season performance.
- Owner history reads saved season reviews.
- Future exit meetings save their context; old unavailable details remain
  unavailable instead of borrowing today's age, grade and contract.
- Team profiles use the current planning cap year and retain future commitment
  information where it represents a different year.

Team and development
- Bought This Year counts dated purchases for the displayed season.
- Career shows every retained season.
- Snap shares use exact recorded unit opportunities and historical team/role
  attribution. Team-stint and full-team-season percentages are distinguished.
- Injury records are no longer mislabeled as games missed.
- New games retain exact snap/roster evidence for both teams. Old ambiguous
  records show unavailable information instead of guessed denominators.
- Participation is laid out in compact rows on player cards.

League and Draft
- Statistical tables expose all retained players with sorting, searching and
  pagination. Transactions search/filter the full retained ledger before paging.
- Coaching history reads the actual saved date and record fields.
- Award summaries include relevant production and properly attributed club
  evidence. Historical byes derive from retained schedules.
- Prospect cards use the board's ranks and round estimates.
- Senior Bowl labels require attendance evidence; rank displacement alone is
  insufficient. Upcoming draft years and visit-stage wording are corrected.
- Auto Pick explains saved priorities followed by GM scouting/roster judgment.
  The board selector controls browsing/manual selection. Decision policy and
  the deliberate developer class export remain unchanged.
- Results describes the draft-day evidence actually shown.

Gameplay
- Game Day uses settled conversion evidence for third and fourth downs.
- Red-zone opportunities require a live opportunity inside the 20; long TDs
  and endgame kneels do not manufacture red-zone possessions.
- Man coverage uses measured pass coverage, excluding unknown observations.
  Recommendation confidence uses that same sample; old unknowns remain unknown.
- Blitz reviews retain yardage cost alongside pressure production.
- Quarterbacks can choose to leave a broken-down pocket before throwing.
  Mobility, coverage, quick answers, pursuit, clock, distance and existing coach
  aggression influence the choice. The separate sack-escape branch remains.

FOOTBALL INTENT
These changes restore consequences and reliable evidence, without prescribing
identical roster or coaching decisions. Public results, role, contract control,
financial flexibility and owner/GM preferences still matter. Waiver priority
and agent refusal are enforced; appropriate cleared signings and later talks
remain possible. Poor short-tenure evidence does not automatically force a
firing. Open receivers and quick answers favor throwing; covered reads and
pressure invite mobile quarterbacks to run; game situation can favor restraint.
