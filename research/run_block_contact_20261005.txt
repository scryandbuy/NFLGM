Run-block execution and short-loss narration - October 5, 2026

Implemented
The existing individual Gaussian block execution draws now supply the
contact-depth variation as well as each block's recorded win/loss. Rating
matchups, support grades, box effects, front fits and after-contact tackling
remain in place. No additional random draw or failure lottery is introduced.
Inside runs weight interior execution twice as heavily as edge execution;
outside runs reverse that relationship. Support roles use their existing
relevance weights. This is coarse path relevance, not tracked coordinates or
an empirical fit of exact block-to-contact geometry. Normalize by the square
root of the sum of squared weights, preserving the original Gaussian contact
variance rather than adding more overall rushing variance or reducing YPC.
No new per-block arrays are stored in game logs or saves.

Fractional negative gains that would round to zero now say "a short loss" for
runs, catches, scrambles and sacks. Whole-yard losses keep their number.
Exact zero remains "no gain". Stored yardage, field position, downs and stats
are unchanged; both field labels can legitimately remain the same.

Controlled checks
101 focused tests passed: block execution/contact, box behavior, support
attributes, actual personnel, blocking awards/opportunities, field positions,
QB designed runs, game-log regressions, scramble contact and goal-line fumbles.
All sampled blocks losing can yield a stuff; a severe individual failure can
disrupt the run while a marginal failure need not erase a positive gain.
Scheme weighting, support failures, unique blockers and original variance are
checked. Narration tests verify short losses without mutating the play.
