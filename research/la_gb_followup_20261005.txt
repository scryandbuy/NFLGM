LA at GB Week 8 follow-up - October 5, 2026

Implemented: late fourth-down field goals
The seven/eight-point deficit exception now requires enough time to kick,
force an ordinary three-and-out, regain possession and attempt a short scoring
drive. The live caller passes actual offensive timeouts, separately from the
timeout edge. The allowance is 4s FG + 5.8s kickoff + 18s three snaps + 9.4s
punt + 30s scoring drive + 40s per unprotected defensive down. This is an
explicit clock-feasibility assumption, not a calibrated win-probability model.
The extraordinary onside-recovery route no longer keeps the generic FG option
alive for this one-score ending. Needed first scores when down 10/11, tying
kicks, earlier bridge kicks and coach discretion among feasible choices remain.

Controlled evidence
The reported fourth-and-20 at opponent31, down8 with28s and no timeouts chose
FG73/100 times before the fix. The new controls exercise both7/8 deficits,
strong kicker, conservative/aggressive coaches and both extremes of the random
choice. All go. A live-drive control attempts fourth down and can fail; it
does not guarantee a conversion or score. At120s, three offensive timeouts
retain a kick-and-stop choice; zero do not. At270s the option remains.
41 focused fourth-down, pace, box-contact and scramble-contact tests passed.

Investigation: designed rushing losses
Read existing frozen0b4c49c records, not new games: 1,632 regular-season games,
83,763 designed runs excluding sneaks. Raw losses7,832 (9.35%); rounded losses
3,797 (4.53%); 4,035 small losses round to zero. Thus the log's1/58 visible
losses does not establish a league-wide shortage of all negative outcomes.
The box correction is present: heavier boxes worsen contact and preserve losses.
Current paired synthetic controls at a six-man box: neutral blocking56/1,000
raw losses, better blocking8/1,000, better front345/1,000. Those deliberately
wide matchup controls are sensitivity tests, not realistic league averages.
Recorded individual block-win draws are separate from penetration: forcing
0/9 versus9/9 recorded wins with other draws fixed yields the same4.6YBC and
16.4yards. Ratings do affect the run, but individual sampled block failures
do not cause penetration. This is a model/accounting disconnect to review,
not evidence that all blocking attributes are disconnected.

Investigation: scramble finishing
Current resolver adjusts a gamma distance draw for QB mobility and mean
on-field pursuit. If it reaches the goal, the play scores before individual
contact selection. At6yards, neutral synthetic QB/defenders score489/1,000
conditional scramble opportunities;469 of those scores precede an individual
contact contest. At16yards,94 touchdowns,86 preceding contact. These are NOT
per-dropback rates, actual Love outcomes or a calibrated real-world comparison.
Better pursuit reduces scores, but this shortcut remains. The separate
terminal chase in plays.resolve_yards_after is NOT the scramble resolver;
fixing that chase alone will not change these scramble touchdowns.

Investigation: GB third-and-six, up8 at opponent17,2:00
Leading offenses bypass end_of_half_plan. The ordinary caller incorporates
score/time/down/personnel and field position; third-and-six deliberately
removes the extra late run bias to preserve attempts to seal the game.
It does not explicitly value a following made FG creating an11-point lead
or use the opponent's exact remaining timeouts in its run/pass choice.
Passing here is not automatically wrong, but this is a concrete situational
decision gap. No change to that choice in this patch.

Running watch list / next actions

   inspect actual saved ratings/box contexts before any overall rush tuning.
2. Design actual scramble pursuit/goal-line arrival opportunities, preserving
   legitimately untouched scores and pre-goal fumble/injury contacts. Validate

3. Add the next-kick/clock-security tradeoff to leading late-down decisions;
   retain reasonable aggressive conversion attempts.
4. Combined integration and later scoring/pace validation remain necessary.
   This patch changes only the late fourth-down kick choice; rushing,
