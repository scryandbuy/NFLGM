Football motivation and restraint
The former greedy matcher always allocated edges/interiors before off-ball
rushers. A reachable inside linebacker could go unblocked while an opposite
tackle was assigned across the formation. Protection now assigns reachable
jobs together, with distinctions between BOB, half slide and full slide.
Slide direction comes from the visible front before actual rush selection.
Keeping a sixth blocker can solve a six-man pressure but costs a receiving
route. A real overload can leave an edge or linebacker hot; neither family
is guaranteed to solve every pressure. Player ratings still execute the job.
Normal four-man front assignments and run-block matching are preserved.
Spare blockers must address reachable free rushers before helping, while
unreachable wide rushers no longer prohibit all interior help.

Confirmed corrections
- Actual unblocked defenders activate the quick-answer logic even when
  blocker/rusher counts are equal.
- Wet conditions affect fumble occurrence once, rather than the defense's
  chance of recovering an already loose ball. Dry behavior is unchanged.
- Late sack escapes acquire a real contact defender for tackling, hit power
  and forced-fumble credit, without retaining provisional sack credit.
- Drops and interceptions remain eligible for live-play injury exposure.
  They previously skipped that branch despite blocking and contact.

sack rates. No positional bonus or player-name exception was added.

Real-world evidence and limits
Primary 2024 nflverse play-by-play: 1,060 scrambles/7 QB fumbles (0.66%);
778 direction-filtered non-scramble QB rushes/11 fumbles (1.41%); 1,308 sacks/
161 QB fumbles (12.31%). The non-scramble category still includes sneaks and
is not a pure film-charted designed-run category. One season is insufficient
to assign exact player-level targets to future generated rosters.
https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/download/pbp/play_by_play_2024.csv.gz
The SIS-authored designed-run injury study reports roughly one injury per246
such runs in its older sample, excluding sneaks/kneels/scrambles. Its injury
definition is not interchangeable with our at-least-one-week missed endpoint.
https://www.sharpfootballanalysis.com/analysis/designed-quarterback-runs-lamar-jackson/
Protection responsibility reference: coach Brandon Behnfeldt, six-man slide.
