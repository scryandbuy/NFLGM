QB scramble workload and containment follow-up — October 5, 2026

Scope / football rationale
Mobile quarterbacks should weigh the open throw, actual escape defenders,
distance/clock, and their coach's willingness to expose them. Running consumes
more work than standing in the pocket. A good edge on one side should not seal
the other side; blocked edges cannot contribute as though already disengaged.
Coaches retain different run aggressiveness and starter protection preferences.
No named-player exceptions, positional sack bonuses, global passing/YAC changes,
or held fatigue candidate dcf3ae6 are included.

Implemented
- Voluntary scrambles and sack escapes charge the existing HB-minus-QB workload
  difference using the selected quarterback's stamina and road effort. No extra
  participation/snap is counted. Designed carries are not charged twice.
- Resolve sack escapes before observation and injury checks. Preserve pressure
  and protection evidence, discard provisional sack credits on an escape.
- Two-point scrambles also receive running workload. Pre-snap retries do not
  count as physical work; actual played/replayed attempts each do.
- Both escape paths receive compact left/right evidence from actual on-field
  edge assignments, arrival availability, pursuit/recognition/speed/acceleration,
  and the coverage side assigned to a dropped edge. Middle drops do not own both
  outside lanes. Open/fully blocked lanes remain neutral, not automatic bonuses.
- Existing open-throw, quick-answer, clock, fourth-down and coach gates remain.

Protection review — remains a separate football decision issue
Primary pairing always assigns tackles to edges, then interiors, then off-ball
rushers. Protection labels influence help/timing but not primary pair allocation.
In prior4,800 equal-athlete protection races, a six-rusher/five-blocker look gave
LB672/674 raw sacks; adding a sixth blocker reduced LB to10/58. Raw races exclude
hot answers, final throws and escapes. This explains a structural opportunity
advantage; it does not justify directly nerfing linebacker/DT sack rates.
Three-interior base fronts also legitimately create more DT rush opportunities.
Next protection work should model who the slide accounts for, the free/hot
defender and reachable assignments, preserving coaching disagreement and help
tradeoffs. Do not automatically drop a nose tackle merely to favor two edges.

Unresolved / not claimed fully calibrated
- Arrival time is a proxy for disengagement, not tracked leverage, rush overshoot
  or an explicit contain call. The availability window and bounded grade slope
  are football modeling assumptions, not estimated real-world coefficients.
- HB-equivalent carry workload remains the existing game design; research does
  not establish that exact fatigue amount for quarterbacks.
- Keep monitoring QB rushing volume, scoring, sack roles and fatigue.16games
  cannot establish injury/fumble rates or long-term workload balance.
- Fumble base rates, contact/slide/out-of-bounds injury distinctions were not
  recalibrated here. The real-world comparison supports separating them and
  measuring actual simulated outcomes by QB/ball security before changing rates.
- No multi-season run, public deployment or push performed.
