FOOTBALL RULE / DESIGN
A completed or intercepted pass requires an inbounds catch. The end line
is ten yards beyond the goal line. A location on or beyond it cannot become
a defensive return. Legal end-zone interceptions may still be touchbacks
or returns. This is field legality, independent of coaching preferences.
No global interception, return, completion or scoring coefficient changes.
2026 NFL Rulebook, Rules1-1-1 and8-1-3:
https://static.www.nfl.com/image/upload/fl_attachment/league/tqivdkzt9mu6wdgsh1ku.pdf

IMPLEMENTATION
game.py supplies exact field_yardline before ordinary and two-point play
resolution. plays.py keeps the existing air draw and tests the unrounded
catch coordinate before invoking the return resolver. On/beyond-end-line
results become incomplete, with end_line_incomplete and out_of_bounds
metadata; no defender interception/return credit, no forced pass breakup,
and no fabricated intentional-throwaway/grounding intent. Protection,
pressure, target and play evidence remain available. Legal catches retain
the existing distribution and return model; fractional catch spots now use
the actual field location consistently. ticker.py describes the incompletion.
