Longitudinal construction method
study_coaching_adaptation.py forces three incoming coaching identities through
the immediate hire path, then uses real construction modules for successive
offseasons. No games, production, injury, XP, retirement, awards or owner feedback.
Initial rosters are normalized to53. Draft order is explicitly alphabetical,
not fabricated standings, and each draft must select224 players. Retention,
tags, FA, trades, drafts, UDFA, cutdown, waivers and PSfill are included.
There is no unchanged-coach control, so trends are descriptive, not causal.
