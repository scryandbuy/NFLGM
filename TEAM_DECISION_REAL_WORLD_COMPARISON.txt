WHAT THE COMPARISON CHANGES

1. The expensive acquisition sequence is the strongest market concern.
Miami's simulated same-round SS contracts ($16.34m and$29.10m APY, both3y)
followed by cutting the first before a game deserve scrutiny. Neither player
declined. Both initially added package value, but the team could not sustain
the combined commitments. The final cut can be rational after overspending;
that does not make the preceding spending plan coherent.
Code rechecks marginal roster gain after signings but leaves the original
offer price/term intact. Test joint role allocation and a feasible funded
roster plan, including pending offers and future contract exposure. Do not
automatically ban two acquisitions carrying the same position label.

Real counterexample to a blanket position ban: Green Bay combined McKinney
with three drafted safeties in2024, with rookies competing alongside him and
learning complementary assignments. That is coherent depth and succession.

2. A recent-signing cut is not automatically unrealistic.
Buffalo's official ledger records O.J. Howard signed in March2022 for one year
and released in August. Green Bay released March2025 signing Mecole Hardman
at cutdown. These establish exceptions, not their league-wide frequency.

4. Final feasibility is a firm game requirement.
Temporary over-cap after drafting is permitted by the user's workflow.
Teams may evaluate the rookie class, then cut/restructure. Do not impose a
hard draft-time cap veto simply to prevent intermediate negative numbers.
In first tested offseason, cleanup leaves DEN36 in one seed; DEN23,NO38,KC50
in the other. The36-player DEN roster actually reaches Week1 kickoff.
That is a failed final recovery, not an acceptable temporary draft state.
Primary2028 and2029 recover all32 teams to53/cap-compliant rosters;2028 WAS
lacks LS, whereas all2029 starting jobs are filled. Retain successful paths.
Ordinary active-roster additions also cause >53 at169/1140 kickoffs. These
counts exclude the separate temporary PS elevation list; they are not legal
54/55 elevations. Repair the trade/roster gate and verify those cases directly.
